Saikaz Barber Shop

Saikaz Barber Shop is a haircut shop in Jabang, cutting hair for both kids and adults. We also offer home service and appointment booking.

Services

We offer haircuts and hair tinting, and you can choose to visit the shop or have us come to you.

  • Haircuts
    • Taper
    • Simple cut
  • Hair tint
  • Home service

Prices

Prices are in dalasi and depend on the service you choose. Contact us for the price of home service.

  • Kids haircut: D50
  • Adults haircut: D100
  • Hair tint: D500

How to book

Booking an appointment saves you waiting time, and it works for both shop visits and home service. Call us on 835824704.

  1. Call us to choose a day and time.
  2. Tell us the service you want and whether you prefer the shop or home service.
  3. Confirm the price with us.
  4. Arrive at the shop, or wait for us at home, at your booked time.

Find us

We are located in Jabang and open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 10am to 11pm. Walk-ins are welcome, and home service is available on request.
