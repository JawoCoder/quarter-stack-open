Your First Week at University

Welcome

This guide is for new students starting their first week. It covers what to sort out before day one, what to expect each day, and who to ask when something goes wrong.

You do not need to do everything at once. Follow the steps in order and you will be fine.

Before day one

  • Set up your university login and email
  • Download your timetable
  • Collect your student ID card
  • Find your first lecture room on the map

Week plan

  1. Monday: registration and welcome talk
  2. Tuesday: meet your course tutor and classmates
  3. Wednesday: library induction and book borrowing
  4. Thursday: first lectures begin
  5. Friday: societies fair

Who to ask

For course questions

  • Your course rep
  • Your personal tutor

For everything else

  • IT help desk: login and wifi problems
  • Student services: housing, money and wellbeing
