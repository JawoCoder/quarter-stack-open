How to Apply for a National ID Card

This page explains how to apply for a national ID card for the first time, from preparing your documents to collecting the finished card.

Before you go

Applying is quicker if you prepare in advance. Check that you are eligible and go to the nearest immigration office .

Check your eligibility

You must be a citizen,old enough to hold an ID card, and not already have a valid card. Confirm the minimum age with your local registration office.

Book an appointment

some offices let you book a time slot online or by phone. Booking helps you avoid long queues on the day.

What to bring

Offices will turn you away if a document is missing, so gather everything the night before.

Payment for the fee

Bring enough money to pay the application fee, since most offices accept cash. Ask for a receipt after you pay, because you will need it to collect your card.

Proof of identity

Bring your birth certificate or a parent's ID card or their birth certificate if you are applying for the first time.

Passport photos

Bring two recent passport-sized photos with a plain background.

At the office

Arrive early and take a queue number. When you are called to a counter, hand in your documents and photos and have your fingerprints taken.

After you apply

You will receive a receipt with a reference number. Keep it safe, because you will need it to collect your card once it is ready.
