What to Do When the Power Comes Back On

This sheet is for anyone at home or in a shop after a power cut. Follow it when the electricity returns, because the first minutes can send a surge through your appliances.

What you need

  • A torch or phone light
  • Dry hands and a dry floor
  • A surge protector or UPS for expensive devices, if you have one

Steps

  1. While the power is still off, unplug TVs, computers, chargers and the AC. Leave one light switched on so you know when power returns.
  2. Never touch a socket, switch or plug with wet hands. Dry your hands and check the floor first.
  3. When the light comes on, wait five minutes before plugging anything in.
  4. Plug in one appliance at a time, starting with the fridge.
  5. Switch on the AC last.

If something goes wrong

If you smell burning or see sparks, switch off the main switch and leave the appliance alone. Never pour water on an electrical fire. Call the fire service, and report faults to NAWEC.
