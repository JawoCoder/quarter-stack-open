## What I asked

I asked AI to help me examine an AI-generated tailoring shop website and identify defects that an HTML validator might not detect.

## What I got

AI helped me identify problems with headings, image descriptions, lists, accessibility, currency, opening hours and unsupported business information.

## What I changed and why

I created the original page, compared it with today’s AI output, improved the prompt and corrected the HTML. I also documented the defects and added TODO comments for information that needs confirmation from the shop owner.

## What I learned

I learned that passing HTML validation does not guarantee that a website is accurate, accessible or ready for publication. I also learned that AI can invent information when important details are not provided.
